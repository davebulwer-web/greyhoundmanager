How to play

The idea

You run a small greyhound kennel. Buy dogs, train them, race them at your home track, breed your own, win the big competitions and bet on any meeting. Every dog has hidden abilities you never see directly: you judge them from times, form and your trainer's stars.

A night's racing

Two tracks race each night. Open a meeting to see the racecards. Each dog shows its recent form lines (newest first), a short write-up and its price. Place bets on the bet slip, then press Off they go! When you're finished, press Next day. Any races you didn't watch are run for you and your bets are settled.

Reading the form

Sec is the time to the first bend: quick starters lead early. Bends shows the dog's place at each bend, so 6421 means it came from last to win. CalTm is the time corrected for the going, so you can compare runs on different nights. The seed letter shows where a dog likes to run: (R) rail, (M) middle, (W) wide. Wide runners hate being drawn in trap 1, and railers hate trap 6.

Your dogs

Every dog you enter gets a race at your track once it has had 5 days' rest. Racing costs fitness, which comes back overnight, fastest on Rest. Special training improves the abilities it works on, but fitness comes back more slowly. Each dog has a natural limit it can't train past. Below 60 fitness a dog runs slower.

Money

Every runner earns a £25 appearance fee, and the first three earn prize money: more in better grades and much more in competition finals. Bills are £4 a day per racing dog, £2 for pups and retired dogs, and £2 extra for special training.

Seasons, retiring and breeding

Every 120 days a new season starts and every dog is a year older. Two and three year olds still improve, five year olds slow down, and dogs retire at six. You can also retire a dog early from the Kennel. Retired bitches can be bred from, using your own retired dogs (free) or top dogs at your track (a stud fee). Pups are born 21 days after mating, carry your kennel prefix, take after their parents with some luck, and are ready to race 60 days later.

Competitions

Each season every track holds a competition. Enter up to 6 dogs before the heats. 24 dogs run in 4 heats, and the first 3 in each go through to 2 semi-finals. The first 3 in each semi make the final, which has big prizes. Your dogs run there whichever track it's at.
